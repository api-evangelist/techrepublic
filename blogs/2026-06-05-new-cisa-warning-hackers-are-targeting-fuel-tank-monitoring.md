Cybercriminals are actively targeting Automatic Tank Gauge (ATG) systems that monitor fuel storage tanks across the United States, according to a new warning from CISA and partner agencies. Attackers are exploiting vulnerabilities like authentication bypass and hardcoded credentials to gain administrative access to these systems. CISA recommends that operators immediately disable internet exposure, strengthen authentication, apply security patches, and enhance monitoring to protect these critical infrastructure components.
