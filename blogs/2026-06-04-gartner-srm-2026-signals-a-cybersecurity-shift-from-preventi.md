Gartner's 2026 Security and Risk Management Summit revealed a fundamental shift in cybersecurity strategy: organizations must abandon the unrealistic goal of preventing all breaches and instead focus on building resilience to quickly recover from incidents. Key concerns include AI agents as security vulnerabilities, identity management gaps in autonomous systems, and the critical importance of enforcing security controls at the data layer rather than relying on model guardrails or network perimeters.
