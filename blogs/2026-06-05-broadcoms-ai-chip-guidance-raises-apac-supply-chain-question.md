Broadcom's disappointing AI chip revenue guidance caused its stock to plummet 12.6%, triggering a broader market selloff in South Korea that hit Samsung and SK Hynix particularly hard. The companies, which supply high-bandwidth memory critical to AI infrastructure, face investor concerns about whether margin and capacity expectations were already priced into their valuations. Enterprise customers testing cheaper AI alternatives like DeepSeek are also creating uncertainty around future infrastructure spending.
