Scam compounds in Southeast Asia are leveraging AI, malware, and automation to execute sophisticated fraud operations that threaten APAC organizations. These criminal networks use AI-generated deepfakes, voice cloning, synthetic identities, and multilingual chatbots to personalize attacks and steal credentials. Security teams must now defend against these threats across account creation, mobile devices, payment systems, and employee awareness.
