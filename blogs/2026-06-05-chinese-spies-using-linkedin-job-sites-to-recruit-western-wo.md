Five Eyes intelligence agencies have warned that Chinese operatives are running a coordinated espionage campaign using fake recruiter profiles on LinkedIn, Indeed, and Upwork to target Western workers with access to sensitive defense and policy information. The scheme involves posing as HR staff to solicit information through interviews and paid trial assignments, with payments ranging from hundreds to thousands of dollars via PayPal, cryptocurrency, and other methods. Targets include security clearance holders, government employees, military personnel, academics, and journalists.
