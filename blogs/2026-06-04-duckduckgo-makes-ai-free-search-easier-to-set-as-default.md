DuckDuckGo has released new browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox that make its AI-free search the default setting, allowing users to avoid AI-generated results. Traffic to DuckDuckGo's No AI page has tripled since Google's recent AI search expansion, raising important questions for IT teams about when AI-generated search results should be enabled, optional, or restricted in regulated workflows.
