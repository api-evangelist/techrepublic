Apple plans to launch a significantly upgraded Siri with iOS 27 in September 2026, leveraging Google's Gemini AI models and Nvidia's Blackwell GPUs to handle complex requests through cloud infrastructure. The hybrid approach maintains simpler tasks on-device while routing demanding queries to Google Cloud, where advanced processing occurs before results return to Apple devices. This partnership represents a strategic shift for Apple, which typically controls its entire technology stack independently.
