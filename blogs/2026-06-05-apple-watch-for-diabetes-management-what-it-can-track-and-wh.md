Apple Watch can help manage diabetes by displaying continuous glucose monitor (CGM) readings, tracking sleep and heart health, sending medication reminders, and allowing users to share health data with providers. However, the device currently depends on external CGM sensors rather than built-in glucose monitoring, though Apple is reportedly developing needle-free glucose tracking technology that could arrive in future models.
