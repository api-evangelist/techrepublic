Microsoft patched a critical flaw in six Android apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Microsoft Loop, and Copilot) that allowed other installed applications to steal account authentication tokens without user permission. IT teams should verify their organizations have deployed the May 2026 patches and review mobile device security policies, particularly for unmanaged devices.
