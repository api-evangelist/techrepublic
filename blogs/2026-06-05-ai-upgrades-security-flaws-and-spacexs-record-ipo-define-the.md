This week in tech saw major advances in AI deployment across devices and workflows, with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and others launching smarter assistants and on-device models. However, significant security vulnerabilities emerged simultaneously, including critical flaws in GitHub's VS Code, web servers, Android systems, and AI-powered applications that could enable token theft, account hijacking, and phishing attacks. The week also featured SpaceX's record-breaking $75 billion IPO announcement and various corporate acquisitions aimed at expanding AI capabilities.
