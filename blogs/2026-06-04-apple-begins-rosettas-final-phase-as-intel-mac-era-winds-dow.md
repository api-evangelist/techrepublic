Apple has announced that macOS 26 Tahoe will be the final major operating system release supporting Intel Macs, with macOS 27 requiring Apple silicon hardware exclusively. The company is also phasing out Rosetta, the translation layer enabling Intel apps to run on Apple silicon, with full, general-purpose support ending after macOS 27, though a limited version will persist for older games.
