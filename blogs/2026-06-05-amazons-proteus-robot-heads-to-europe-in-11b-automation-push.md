Amazon is expanding its robotics deployment across Europe with a 10 billion euro investment, introducing an upgraded Proteus robot equipped with natural-language capabilities alongside new robots STARK and Vulcan. The company plans to add 25,000 European employees while rolling out these automated systems to handle physically demanding warehouse tasks, positioning the technology as complementary to human workers rather than replacements.
