DentaQuest, a major dental benefits provider serving 35 million customers, confirmed a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized network access that exposed data for approximately 2.6 million accounts. The leaked dataset, which surfaced on a public breach listing by threat actors ShinyHunters, includes sensitive personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, government IDs, and health insurance details. The company stated that its systems remain operational and it is working with cybersecurity experts and law enforcement to determine the full scope of the breach.
