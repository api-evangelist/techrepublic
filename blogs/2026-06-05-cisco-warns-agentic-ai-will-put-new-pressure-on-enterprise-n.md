Cisco executives warn that agentic AI systems will triple network traffic over the next three years, creating significant infrastructure and security challenges for enterprises. Unlike chatbots with sporadic usage patterns, autonomous AI agents operating at machine speed generate sustained, high-volume demands that require substantial upgrades to data center, workplace, and service provider networks. The company is promoting solutions like Cisco IQ to help IT teams monitor and secure their increasingly complex AI-powered infrastructure.
